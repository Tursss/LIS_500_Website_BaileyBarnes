Spencer Bailey

Education:

Bachelor of Science (BS), Computer Sciences, Data Science, Minor(s): Graphic Design, University of Wisconsin, Madison

Master of Science (MS), Information (In-Progress), University of Wisconsin, Madison

Biography:

I'm Spencer Bailey, and I am a part-time master's student at UW Madison in the Information program. Outside of my education I am a full-time Software and Data Engineer for UW Madison's Division of Extension. In my role I support colleagues statewide by making data accessible and actionable. I build and design data-driven applications/pipelines/workflows, as well as steward business critical data collection systems.

Prior to my current role, I was employed in UW Madison's Job Rotation Program where I was one of the first four IT rotators ever in the program. During my time there I rotated through four unique units on campus and learned new and interesting tools and technologies in each. Programs like this are unique and highly beneficial to early career professionals.

Interests:

Outside of work and school I enjoy spending my time cooking for friends, and getting outside to walk, run, hike, ski (on water and snow), snowboard, or just hang out. I love all kinds of games (video games, board games, and card games alike). I also like spending time with my two cats - Chun Li and Kiki.

Why LIS 500:

I am taking this class as suggested by my advisors in order to graduate here in December. Aside from that, I also see great value in this class as I continue in my work. I am currently the one of very few developers on my very small team of technology support staff. Further, I am the sole provider of my service supporting custom application development, so I need to make sure that the tools I develop are accessible, and that they comply with federal requirements on accessibility. I also would hate for my work to have any unintended disparaging effects on my users, and this class I am hoping will equip me will tools I need to prevent this.

Tech Specific Interests:

I enjoy writing code in tons of languages. I am proficient in JavaScript, R, HTML (though not really a coding language), Python, PHP, and am pretty good at learning language conventions on the fly.

I am also interested in digital art apps. I use a Surface Pro as my personal device and I really enjoy using it (and the stylus) to make digital art with tools like Adobe PhotoShop, Illustrator, and Fresco.
